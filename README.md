# WeekendKit — Make This Weekend Count in Bengaluru

A Sunday planner for Bengaluru: real things happening **today, Sunday 22 November 2026** — a Shreya Ghoshal concert tonight, a Lucknowi food festival, a theatre festival, Sunday brunches and free morning walks — plus a build-your-day planner, six day trips, a weekend budget calculator and a packing list.

**Live:** https://abhiramunni.github.io/weekendkit/

## What it does

- **This weekend, live** — 12 verified picks for Sunday 22 Nov 2026 (Shreya Ghoshal live 6 PM at District Arena, Yelahanka; Girish Karnad Theatre Festival; Lucknowi Food Festival; The Sunday Table brunch ₹1,800; Toon Town Brunch ₹3,000; SHAAM-E-RAFI-2; Yuvan X Harris tribute; free Colour Walk, Art Picnic and Long Run; go-karting; Sushi O'Clock). Each pick shows a live status — Happening now / Later today / Done for today — computed from the real clock.
- **Tonight strip** — live ticking countdown to the 6 PM concert, with the honest travel note: leave BTM by 4 PM.
- **My day builder** — morning / afternoon / evening slots, one pick each. Add a pick and it lands in the right slot; the running per-person total updates live, with "at listing" flagged where the price lives on the ticket page.
- **Day trips** — Nandi Hills, Bannerghatta Safari, Lepakshi, Mysuru, Shivanasamudra Falls and Coorg, with distance, drive time and per-person budget. Save trips and see the group cost.
- **Budget calculator** — group size × per-person spend, split into food (40%), tickets (35%) and transport (25%).
- **Packing list** — 8 items, tick-off progress ring, saved on device.
- **Guides** — six plain-language Sunday savers (last metro 11 PM, book before you leave, leave by 4 PM, carry cash, free things need timing, brunch means book a table).
- **Weather strip** — today's Bengaluru forecast: 28°C now, high 29° / low 17°, 0% rain, sunset 5:49 PM, AQI 139.

## Details

- Single self-contained HTML file (plus two photos). No backend, no signup, no tracking — everything saves in `localStorage` on your device.
- Warm editorial design (ivory paper, espresso ink, bronze and terracotta), Fraunces + Inter, cinematic sun-rise intro with tap-to-skip (quick on return visits), Ken Burns drift on the photos, 3-step onboarding coach.
- Copy your plan as text, remove the sample plan, or erase everything with a two-tap confirm.
- Listings and prices were checked from public event pages on 22 Nov 2026; ticket prices change and shows sell out — check the listing before you leave.
